

\# Changelog



\## 0.2.0 - Initial development release



\### Added

\- Main printer dashboard.

\- Print thumbnail and progress.

\- Pause, Resume, Cancel and Emergency Stop.

\- Custom printer actions.

\- Custom service calls.

\- Statistics tiles.

\- Multiple camera support.

\- Offline printer view.

\- Animated sleeping printer illustration.

\- Visual configuration editor.

\- Automatic entity discovery.

\- Per-instance printer configuration.

\- Polish and English translations.

\- Responsive Home Assistant styling.

\- Initial unit tests.



\### Known limitations

\- Automatic entity discovery uses naming heuristics.

\- The user must verify detected entities.

\- Camera support depends on Home Assistant camera/image entities or compatible image URLs.

\- The card does not connect directly to Moonraker.
